Delete your Molehill Madness account

Molehill Madness, published by Inthrall. This page is how to have your account and everything held against it removed.

What the account is

You never made one, and that is not an oversight. The first time you play with people who are not in the room, the game asks our service for an account and gets back a random identifier and a secret, both of which live on your device. There is no username, no password, and no sign in. The account holds an age band, when it was created, when it was last seen, and an email address only if an adult chose to add one.

Playing on the couch with people in the same room creates nothing and sends nothing, so there is nothing to delete.

How to ask

Email hello-mole@decryptic.app with the subject Delete my account.

Because the account is a random identifier rather than a name, we need something that ties the request to it. Either of these is enough:

  • Write from the email address linked to the account, if one was added.
  • Tell us a game code you played in and roughly when. A code is the five letters shown when a match is set up.

Without one of those we cannot tell which account is yours, and we will not guess, because guessing means deleting somebody else's.

What gets deleted

  • The account: its identifier, its secret, its age band, and its created and last seen times.
  • Any email address linked to it, and any unfinished attempt to link one.
  • Any place in the matchmaking queue.
  • The matches it took part in, including the plans it submitted and any emotes it sent.

All of it, permanently, with nothing kept back. We hold no backups of it that outlive the deletion, and nothing about the account is retained for legal or accounting reasons, because none of it was ever a transaction.

Requests are dealt with within 30 days, and usually far sooner. We will reply to say it is done.

Deleting the game instead

Uninstalling removes everything held on the device: the account identifier, the secret, the settings, and any match in progress. It does not by itself remove the account from our service, so ask us as well if that is what you want.

More detail

Everything the game holds and why is set out in the privacy policy, which is written against the service's own database rather than from a template.
